# About Me

I am a last year aerospace engineering student at *Universidad Carlos 3 de Madrid*. I am also a self-taught software developer with professional experience in digital signal processing, embedded systems, flight software, and full-stack web development. I used to be part of the rocketry team at my university, however in the latter years I found that to be taking too much of my time making it hard to finish my degree. Currently I am working to specialize into navigation, particularly, my thesis (still in progress) is about GNSS+IMU sensor fusion. As part of this work I have contributed to the [GNSS-SDR](https://github.com/gnss-sdr/gnss-sdr) project.

Outside my professional life, I enjoy writting software for fun and taking pictures of the night sky. You can check out my [software projects](https://castlecodes.eu/projects) as well as my somewhat decent attempts at deep sky [astrophotography](https://castlecodes.eu/astro). Sometimes I attempt to hunt know comets with my DSLR camera, usually without success, but I find the process satisfying nontheless.
